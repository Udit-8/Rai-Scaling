Common questions

Straight answers.

Is the ad spend included in the £197?

No. Your ad spend is separate and paid directly to Meta from your own card, in your own ad account. You can start from £5 a day, but we recommend £10 a day or more for local services.

Do you take a cut of my ad spend?

Never. You pay us £197 a month flat, whatever you spend on ads.

Is there a contract?

No. It's monthly rolling, and you can cancel any time.

How does the 14-day money-back guarantee work?

If you decide it's not for you within 14 days of your first payment, message us and we'll refund your £197 fee. Money already spent on ads goes to Meta, not us, so we can't refund that part.

Can you guarantee a number of leads?

No, and you should be careful of anyone who does. Results depend on your offer, area, budget and how quickly you follow up. What we promise is the work: good ads, weekly optimisation and honest monthly reporting on spend, leads and cost per lead.

Who owns the ad account?

You do. Everything runs in your own Meta ad account and you give us partner access. We never ask for your password. If you leave, you keep the account, the ads, the data and the leads.

Do I need to make the ads myself?

No. We make the images and short videos, and write the copy, using your job photos. You approve every ad before it goes live, and revisions are unlimited.

How quickly can my ads go live?

Usually within about a week of signing up. It depends on how quickly you approve the ads and on Meta's own ad review.

Where do my leads go?

Leads fill in a short form with qualifying questions, then go straight into your own GoHighLevel CRM. That's included, along with missed-call text-back and automated review requests, so you get notified and nothing gets lost.

When can I reach you?

On WhatsApp during UK hours, Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm. You also get a 20-minute review call every month.

What do you need from me?

A Facebook Page, a few photos of your work, a 15-minute onboarding call, quick approvals on WhatsApp and, most importantly, fast replies to your leads.

Do you offer anything beyond ads?

Yes. We build conversion landing pages, AI lead follow-up and appointment setting, and Google Business Profile and review campaigns. These are billed separately from the £197 plan. Ask us about them on a call.
