FAQ

Questions coaches ask us

How long until I get calls?

We aim to have your ads live within about 7 days of your offer session. The guarantee runs for 45 days from launch.

How much of my time does this take?

A 90-minute offer session, about an hour in week 1, approving ads within 48 hours, a 30-minute weekly review, and taking the calls we book. We handle the rest.

Is this done-for-you or done-with-you?

Both. We write and run the ads, build the filter and set up follow-up for you. We shape the offer and review the numbers with you, because you know your clients best.

Who pays for the ads, and how much?

You do, directly to Meta from your own ad account: at least ₹1,000 a day. That money never passes through us, and the account stays yours.

What counts as a “qualified” call?

Someone who passed all three gates (they can afford your program, they make the decision, and they want to start within 30 days) and who actually shows up to the call.

What happens after the 45 days?

You keep everything we built. If you want us to keep running and improving it, we’ll talk through the options before the 45 days end. There’s no lock-in.

What happens after I book a call?

We look at your offer and your current lead flow, find what’s stopping consistent high-ticket clients, and map out your first 45 days. If we can genuinely help, we’ll show you how we’d work together. If not, you keep the plan.

Do I need a team or existing ads?

No. You need a program of ₹30K or more that has already sold to at least 5 clients, and the time to take your calls.
