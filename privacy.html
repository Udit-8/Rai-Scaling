Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains how Rai Scaling (“we”, “us”) collects, uses and protects personal information when you visit raiscaling.com, fill in one of our Facebook or Instagram lead forms, book a call, buy our Meta Ads Done For You plan, or work with us as a client. We are the data controller for the information described here, except where we handle your customers’ leads on your behalf (see section 4).

1. What we collect

  • Contact details: your name, business name, phone or WhatsApp number and email address.
  • Your answers: what you tell us in our forms and on calls, such as your trade, service area, ad budget and how you currently get work.
  • Booking details: the date and time of any call you book, and notes from that call.
  • Purchase details: your plan, payment status, billing name and address. Payments are processed by Whop and its payment partners. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Client materials: job photos, logos, approvals and messages you send us, and access to your Facebook Page and Meta ad account so we can run your ads.
  • Website and ad data: basic usage information (pages visited, device and browser type) collected through cookies and similar tools, including Meta’s advertising tools, to measure and improve our ads.

Please don’t send us bank details, government ID numbers or health information through our forms or WhatsApp.

2. How we use it, and our lawful basis

  • To reply to your enquiry and check we’re a good fit (legitimate interests, or steps you ask us to take before a contract).
  • To schedule your call and send reminders (steps before a contract).
  • To take payment, onboard you and deliver the service: making and running your ads, setting up your lead form and CRM, and supporting you on WhatsApp (performance of our contract with you).
  • To keep business and tax records (legal obligation).
  • To measure and improve our own advertising and website (your consent for non-essential cookies, and legitimate interests).
  • To send you occasional updates about our services (legitimate interests or consent; you can opt out at any time).

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business, under their own privacy and security terms:

  • Meta Platforms (Facebook and Instagram lead forms, ad accounts and advertising tools; WhatsApp for messaging)
  • Whop (checkout, payments and subscription management)
  • GoHighLevel (our booking calendar and CRM, and the CRM we set up for clients)
  • Our email, video-call and cloud storage providers
  • Our website hosting provider

We may also disclose information where the law requires it, or to protect our rights.

4. Leads we handle for clients

When we run ads for you, the people who enquire through your lead forms are your customers. For that information, you are the data controller and we act as your processor: we only use it to run and report on your campaigns, send it to your CRM, and set up the follow-up tools you ask for (such as missed-call text-back and review requests). You are responsible for having your own privacy notice and for how you contact those leads. When our service ends, we remove our access and delete any copies we hold, unless the law requires us to keep them.

5. International transfers

Rai Scaling and some of our providers are based outside the UK, including in the United States and India. When your information is transferred outside the UK, we rely on our providers’ recognised safeguards, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, standard contractual clauses or the UK–US data bridge.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information for up to 24 months after our last contact with you, unless you become a client or ask us to delete it sooner. Client and payment records are kept for as long as needed to deliver our services and for up to 6 years after that to meet legal and tax obligations.

7. Your rights

Under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018) and other applicable laws, you can:

  • Ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or update it
  • Ask us to delete it
  • Ask us to restrict or stop using it, including objecting to marketing
  • Ask us to send it to you or another provider in a portable format
  • Withdraw your consent at any time (this won’t affect anything we did before you withdrew it)

To use any of these rights, email us at the address below. We aim to respond within one month. You can also stop WhatsApp or email messages from us at any time by replying “STOP”.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, please contact us first. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

8. Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar tools, including the Meta Pixel, to understand how visitors use the site and to measure our ads. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and manage ad preferences in your Facebook and Instagram settings.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including access controls and reputable, secure service providers. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to keep your data safe.

10. Children

Our services are for business owners. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows when it last changed.

12. Contact

For questions, requests or complaints about your personal information, contact us at hello@raiscaling.com.
